# Mommy's World

The VRChat home of **Mommy's**, the dance academy and entertainers club. One world for the club's Rave House, its
Dance Academy and more.

**Status: seed.** This repo holds folders and plans only. There is no Unity project yet, on purpose: the world gets
designed first, then built from scratch with [Unity Unicorn](https://github.com/findastra/unity-unicorn)'s tools.

By Astra.

## How it's made

Three things come first in every piece of this world:

- **Creativity.** The ideas and the looks come from Astra and the club. AI assistants help build them. They don't
  decide them.
- **Authenticity.** Everything is made for this world, from scratch. Nothing is copied from other worlds, and any
  inspiration is named as inspiration.
- **Provenance.** Every piece records who made it, how, and with which tools, AI included. Without that record, a
  piece doesn't ship.

How AI is used: [AI-USE.md](AI-USE.md). How every piece is recorded: [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md). Who made
what: [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
