## What we know about Mommy's

- Mommy's is a VRChat dance academy and entertainers club.
- It runs classes, rehearsals and events, which [Mommy's Mommies](https://github.com/findastra/mommys-mommies) plans.
- [Mommy's Website](https://github.com/findastra/mommys-website)'s brief lists an Avatar Academy, a Dance Academy, and
  raves and performances.
- It has a Discord with a role picker, [Role Fairy](https://github.com/findastra/role-fairy).
- The club's brand art (posters, banners, CD art, a "neon kisses" palette) is in Astra's `mommys` folder on her PC.
  None of it is copied here. Astra decides who is credited for each piece, and whether it may be shown.
- The website brief says never to use an inspiration world's links as Mommy's World's.

## What's decided

- The repo is public under findastra. That is Astra's rule for every new world.
- Design comes first. There is no Unity project until Astra says go.
- When Unity starts, the world is built from scratch with Unity Unicorn's tools, from VRChat Creator Companion's World
  template. Nothing is copied from other worlds.
- Every piece is recorded ([PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md)), and AI use is written down
  ([AI-USE.md](../AI-USE.md)).
