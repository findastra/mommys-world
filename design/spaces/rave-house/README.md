# Rave House (the Roof)

Named by Astra on 2026-10-19. On 2026-10-20 she made it the roof, as in her September draft (decision 10).

## Decided

- **Where:** the roof. The draft says: "The roof is the rave floor: pool, minibar, DJ decks, string lights."
- **DJ decks:** beside the pool, under the string lights.
- **Music:** DJs stream live into a video player, and playlists of links too. No songs are stored in the world.
- **Lights:** the whole roof pulses with the music (AudioLink). The dance floor glows and reacts to the music.
- **Effects:** lasers, fog and haze, sparkles, confetti bursts, light beams, and screens with visuals. Each one has
  a switch.
- **Also on the roof:** a bar and lounge corner (the minibar), a VIP balcony, a photo spot, dance platforms, dance
  poles, and a quiet chill corner.
- **Who controls the lights:** anyone.
- **Showcases:** around the pole, so every floor can watch (decision 11).
